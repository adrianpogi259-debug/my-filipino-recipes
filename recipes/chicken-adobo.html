Chicken Adobo

Chicken cooked low and slow in a savory, tangy sauce. Best enjoyed with warm rice.

15 minPrep time
40 minCook time
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)

Directions

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and half of the garlic in a bowl. Let it marinate for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot. Lightly brown the remaining garlic.
  3. Add the chicken and its marinade. Cook for a few minutes, turning the pieces so they are coated.
  4. Pour in the water and add the bay leaves and peppercorns. Bring to a boil.
  5. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  6. Add the vinegar. Let it simmer without stirring for a few minutes, then stir gently.
  7. Taste the sauce and add a little sugar if you prefer. Serve hot with rice.

Cooking tip

Let the sauce simmer until it reaches the flavor and thickness you like. Always check that the chicken is fully cooked before serving.

Serve it with

Steamed rice and a side of fresh vegetables make a simple, satisfying meal.
