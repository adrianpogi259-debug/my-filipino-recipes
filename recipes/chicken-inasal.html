Chicken Inasal

A Filipino grilled chicken dish known for its tangy marinade and golden annatto color.

Chicken Inasal is a well-loved grilled chicken dish from the Visayas. It is marinated with calamansi, vinegar, garlic, and spices, then basted with annatto oil while cooking.

20 minutes + marinatingPreparation
35–45 minutesCooking time
4 peopleServings

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken leg quarters or chicken pieces
  • 3 tablespoons calamansi juice
  • ¼ cup vinegar
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons annatto oil for basting

How to prepare

  1. Mix the calamansi juice, vinegar, garlic, ginger, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Place the chicken in the marinade. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Prepare the grill. Ask an adult to handle the hot grill and charcoal.
  4. Grill the chicken, turning it from time to time. Brush with annatto oil as it cooks.
  5. Check that the chicken is fully cooked before serving. Serve with rice and a dipping sauce.

Kitchen reminder: Ask an adult to supervise cooking and make sure the chicken is fully cooked before serving.
