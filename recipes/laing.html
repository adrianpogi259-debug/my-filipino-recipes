Laing

A Bicolano dish made with dried taro leaves simmered in coconut milk.

Photo by Lokalpedia on Wikimedia Commons.

Laing comes from the Bicol region. It is made by slowly cooking dried taro leaves in coconut milk with garlic, onion, ginger, and chili. The result is a rich and flavorful dish that goes well with rice.

15 minutesPreparation
45–60 minutesCooking time
4 peopleServings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups dried taro leaves
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 cup coconut cream
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, sliced
  • 2–3 chili peppers, optional
  • Salt to taste

How to prepare

  1. Prepare the onion, garlic, and ginger.
  2. In a pot, combine the coconut milk, onion, garlic, and ginger.
  3. Add the dried taro leaves. Let them soften and cook gently without stirring too much at first.
  4. Pour in the coconut cream and continue simmering until the leaves are tender and the sauce thickens.
  5. Season with salt. Add chili if desired, then serve with rice.

Kitchen reminder: Ask an adult for help when using a grill, stove, knife, or hot pan. Make sure meat is fully cooked before eating.

Serving idea
