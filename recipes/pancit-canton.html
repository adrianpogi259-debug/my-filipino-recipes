Pancit Canton

Stir-fried noodles tossed with vegetables and chicken for a colorful, filling dish.

20 minPrep time
20 minCook time
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 250 g pancit canton noodles
  • 200 g chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 carrot, cut into strips
  • 1 cup cabbage, sliced
  • 1/2 cup green beans, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth or water
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Calamansi, for serving

Directions

  1. Prepare and slice all the vegetables and chicken before heating the pan.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pan. Cook the chicken until fully cooked, then set it aside.
  3. In the same pan, sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  4. Add the carrots and green beans. Stir-fry for a few minutes, then add the cabbage.
  5. Pour in the broth and soy sauce. Bring the liquid to a gentle boil.
  6. Add the pancit canton noodles. Toss gently until the noodles absorb most of the liquid and become tender.
  7. Return the chicken to the pan. Season with black pepper and mix well.
  8. Serve warm with calamansi on the side.

Cooking tip

Keep a little broth nearby. Add a splash if the noodles become dry before they are tender.

Make it your own

You can use shrimp or other vegetables, depending on what you have available.
