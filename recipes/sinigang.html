Warm and comforting

Sinigang na Baboy

A Filipino sour soup with tender pork, fresh vegetables, and a flavorful broth.

Photo by Anne Molina, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

15 minPrep time
1 hrCook time
4–5Servings

Ingredients

  • 750 g pork ribs or pork belly, cut into pieces
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bunch kangkong, washed
  • 8–10 pieces string beans, cut into sections
  • 1 packet tamarind soup mix, or tamarind to taste
  • Fish sauce, to taste

Directions

  1. Place the pork and water in a pot. Bring to a boil and skim off any foam that rises to the surface.
  2. Add the onion and tomatoes. Lower the heat and simmer until the pork becomes tender.
  3. Add the radish and string beans. Cook until they begin to soften.
  4. Stir in the tamarind soup mix a little at a time, tasting until the broth is pleasantly sour.
  5. Add the eggplant and cook until tender.
  6. Season with fish sauce according to your taste.
  7. Add the kangkong near the end and cook briefly until wilted. Serve hot.

Cooking tip

Add the leafy vegetables near the end so they keep their color and texture. Adjust the sourness gradually.

Serve it with

Enjoy with hot rice and a small saucer of fish sauce with chili, if you like.
