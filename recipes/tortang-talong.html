Tortang Talong

A Filipino eggplant omelette that is soft inside and golden on the outside.

Photo by Aerous on Wikimedia Commons.

Tortang Talong is a simple Filipino eggplant dish. The eggplant is roasted until soft, peeled, flattened, dipped in beaten egg, and pan-fried until golden.

15 minutesPreparation
15–20 minutesCooking time
2 peopleServings

Ingredients

  • 2 long eggplants
  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • A pinch of ground pepper
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil

How to prepare

  1. Wash the eggplants. Ask an adult to help roast them until the skin is charred and the inside is soft.
  2. Let the eggplants cool, then carefully peel off the skin while keeping the stems attached.
  3. Flatten each eggplant gently with a fork.
  4. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper in a shallow bowl, then coat each eggplant in the mixture.
  5. With adult help, pan-fry each eggplant in a little oil until both sides are golden. Serve with rice.

Kitchen reminder: Ask an adult for help when using a grill, stove, knife, or hot pan. Make sure meat is fully cooked before eating.
